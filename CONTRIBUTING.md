# Contributing to Penstock

Please describe the behavior you changed and how you tested it. You can file issues and discuss ideas without granting rights to any code.

Miguel Rodriguez offers the Community Edition for noncommercial use and handles commercial licensing separately. Contributors keep their copyright. To submit code or documentation, include the following statement in your pull request:

> I have the right to submit this contribution. I grant Miguel Rodriguez a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free copyright license to use, modify, reproduce, distribute and sublicense my contribution under the community license and separate commercial terms.

Identify third-party material and its license separately. Only grant rights you hold. The maintainer should confirm this statement and review any third-party terms before merging a contribution.
